About S+

Made at the window.

S+ Plus Coffee is a coffee trailer on W Main St in downtown Durham. We pull espresso, whisk matcha and brew tea to order.

We also like to experiment, which is how drinks like the D-1 and Orangello happened. Come by, grab a drink, and stay for the good vibes.
